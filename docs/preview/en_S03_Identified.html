How your report is protected

Your report is locked (encrypted) on our server as soon as it arrives. A live-compromised intake server could read what you submit while it is being encrypted.

If the intake server is compromised or legally compelled while you use the website (no-JavaScript) version, what you type, and your passphrase when you log in, can be captured. For the highest risk, use the Candor Source App.

If you are at higher risk

Tor's connection encryption does not yet resist future quantum computers. Someone who records traffic today might read website reports in the future. The Candor app encrypts your report on your device in a way designed to resist this.

Replies on this website
When you sign in on this website, the server looks up your mailbox. A compromised server could note when you sign in and read your replies.

What protects you and what does not
What this site does. When you use Tor Browser, your internet address is hidden from us and from the people who read reports. We don't ask for your name. Our systems keep only the day your report is picked up for the team, not the time. The team sees ⁨only that day⁩. What this site can't do. It can't see or clean your computer or phone. It can't stop your employer or internet provider from seeing that you used Tor, and when. It can't remove every clue from your files or your words. The steps on this page help with those risks. In an emergency, ⁨the General Counsel and the Audit Committee Chair⁩, including someone independent of management, can together give one more person access to a report for up to 8 hours. This is recorded and reviewed. There is no backup key.

Staying safe on this page

No tool can hide you completely. This site cannot hide what your files and words reveal, or what a watched device sees.

If you are at higher risk

On this website version, someone who takes over or legally forces the server while you use it could capture what you type and your passphrase. For the highest risk, use the Candor Source App, downloaded over Tor, ideally on Tails.

Use Tor Browser set to Safest. A VPN or a "private window" in another browser does not protect you in the same way.

If you are at higher risk

Get Tor Browser only from the Tor Project, over Tor if you can, and check its signature. If a yellow JavaScript warning shows on this page, set the security level to Safest before you go on. When you finish, choose New Identity and close the browser.

Don't log in to email, social media or any other account while you use this site.

If you are at higher risk

Logging in links what you do to you. Don't look up the people or the subject of your report from work or personal accounts around the time you report. If you need to look something up, do it in Tor Browser without logging in, and not only right before you send.
