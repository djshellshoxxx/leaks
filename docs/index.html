Anonymous reporting, built carefully.

Candor is a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0) platform for anonymous whistleblowing and secure case management, designed for newsrooms, NGOs, companies and public bodies that need to receive reports from people who cannot afford to be identified.

Work in progress. Candor is not ready for real use and there is no live instance. Nothing on this site accepts reports. No system is unhackable or perfectly anonymous; every protection in the design states what it protects, from whom, and what risk remains.

Design principles

Tor only

Reports arrive only through a Tor onion service. There is no clearnet fallback and the web interface works without JavaScript.

No master key

Reports are encrypted to short-lived, per-recipient keys with hybrid post-quantum encryption (HPKE with X-Wing). Administrators hold no case keys.

Conflict-of-interest aware

Anyone a report concerns is excluded before encryption, so they never hold a key that can open it.

Minimal metadata

No IP addresses, browser details or exact submission times are stored or logged. Audit logs record staff actions, not sources.

Isolated intake

The internet-facing zone holds only ciphertext; the internal case zone pulls from it and nothing flows back.

Audited step by step

Every component is reviewed by an independent auditor before it is integrated; findings and decisions are published in the repository.
