# Candor Wiki

Candor (working name) is an anonymous reporting, source-communication and case-management platform.
This wiki is a navigation layer over the repository. The authoritative material is in `specs/` and
`process/STATUS.md`.

- [User Guide](User-Guide): what can and cannot be used today
- [Developer Guide](Developer-Guide): build, check and test commands
- [Architecture and Design Rationale](Architecture-and-Design-Rationale)
- [Codebase Map](Codebase-Map)

Candor is not "unhackable", "perfectly anonymous" or "untraceable". Each protection is stated with
what it protects, from whom, and under which assumptions (`specs/40-SECURITY-ASSUMPTIONS.md`).
