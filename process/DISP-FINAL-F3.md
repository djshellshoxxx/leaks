- 19 now defines BS-INTAKE as in 09 §5.6 and 08 RL-10 (no envelopes, no replies). Two texts still disagree and should be aligned: 03 ("Z-INTAKE backup = C-08 account records + pending replies") and 35 B6 ("sealed replies" in BS-INTAKE).

- F3 documents do not specify how chaff is discarded at import. 29 ST-169 and 30 AT-088 test only the observable outcome; the mechanism belongs to 04/07.
- 33 §4.1 defines what a `SERVER_RELEASE` entry means; its byte format belongs to the entry list in 04.
- 17 adds host H-ALERT (`alert-relay`, per 06 §4.2), segment N-ALERT and flows F5b/F10a. If 16 §14.2 lists Z-SOC flows, it should include them.
- 32 renames `relay.import_schedule` to `relay.import_slots` and uses the 07 §7.3 values: `intake.chaff.mean_interval` is 2 h and cannot be raised or disabled, and `min_recipients` = 1 is DANGEROUS.
