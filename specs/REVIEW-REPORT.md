# REVIEW-REPORT — Adversarial Review and Revision Record

Status: GENERATED by `tools/review_report.py` from `process/REVIEW-*.md` and `process/DISP-*.md`. Regenerate after any disposition change.

## 1. Method

After the complete v1.0 specification set was written, the lead architect switched roles and commissioned three independent adversarial reviews, each instructed to break the design from a different perspective. Each weakness was recorded with scenario, affected documents, why the spec did not prevent it, a proposed fix and residual risk (full text in `process/REVIEW-A.md`, `REVIEW-B.md`, `REVIEW-C.md`). The lead architect then issued binding revision decisions ADR-034..ADR-047 (`DECISIONS.md` §7) and eight revision groups (G1–G8) plus a final consistency pass (F1–F3) amended every affected document, recording a disposition for every finding that touched their documents (`process/DISP-*.md`). Where a reviewer's proposed fix conflicted with a revision ADR, the ADR prevailed and the difference is noted in the disposition.

Final status per finding = the WEAKEST disposition recorded by any group whose documents it touched (a finding is Fixed only when every affected document fixed its part), with all group dispositions listed. 'Partially fixed' means the design change landed but a documented residual risk remains (see the affected document's Residual risks section and `40-SECURITY-ASSUMPTIONS.md`).
