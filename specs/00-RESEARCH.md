## 7. Anonymity attack literature summary

Categories follow R4: **PRACTICAL** = observed in the wild or feasible today for realistic adversaries; **LABORATORY** = demonstrated experimentally under assumptions often absent in deployment; **THEORETICAL** = analytic/simulation, or capability few adversaries have. The "realistic threat" column is written for a whistleblower submitting to a Candor onion service (C-05).

Adversaries (R4 §1): A employer/reported-on organisation (highest likelihood); B national LE/intel; C global/multi-AS passive; D malicious relay operators (Sybil); E DoS/censor.

| Precedent | SecureDrop, GlobaLeaks (UNVERIFIED docs, well established) | No major whistleblowing platform (UNVERIFIED) | Tor |

Options (R4 §7): **Tor only** — recommended primary; **I2P only** — reject; **Tor + I2P** — reject (doubles attack surface; I2P users get weaker anonymity under the same label; I2P also blockable); **Transport abstraction** — recommended as architecture with admission criteria (match or beat Tor on anonymity set, audit status, client hardening).

**Recommendation (as adopted in ADR-001/002/003):** anonymous mode only via Tor v3 onion service on C-tor ≥0.4.8 with PoW and full vanguards; migrate to Arti when onion services are declared production-ready *with* PoW and full vanguards; onion-only endpoint enforces Tor without fingerprinting; clearnet site information-only (Tor exit-list check in memory, never logged); no clearnet fallback; separate restricted-discovery onion for staff; Transport Adapter for future cover-traffic/mixnet transports; non-network defences mandatory.

## 12. Residual risks and limitations

- **Evidence quality.** A significant share of the evidence is UNVERIFIED (all commercial vendor claims; pre-2020 SD audit contents; GlobaLeaks audit PDFs; ROCA/Efail/MEGA/Nextcloud URLs; insider incidents INC-68..72; Tor Metrics figures; Arti onion-service production status). Designs that rely on these should be re-checked before external publication or procurement statements.
- **Summariser risk.** R1 documents a fully fabricated audit summary produced by an automated summariser. Any evidence not traceable to a primary document or to an in-session extraction should be treated as UNVERIFIED.
- **CVE identifiers** CVE-2026-71863, CVE-2026-70655 and the CVE-2026-35465 version range have single or conflicting sources.
- **Coverage gaps.** Not covered as separate incidents: Terry Albury, Daniel Hale, Jeffrey Sterling, James Wolfe/Ali Watkins records seizure (R3 closing note). Commercial platforms could not be analysed technically at all.
- **Recency.** State of the art is as of 2026-09-30; WEBCAT/WAICT, Arti, EN 301 549 OJ citation, SP 800-218r1, Bill C-36 and CRA steward duties are moving targets.
- **Non-network channels dominate.** The literature shows stylometry, canary traps, printer dots and source behaviour defeat network anonymity in practice; no tool reliably removes content-level fingerprints (R5 D.2). Candor can reduce but not remove these risks.

### Open Issues for ADR revision
- **ADR-005 / ADR-006 (Tier W server-side Argon2id at m=256 MiB):** R5 A.6 notes browsers/WASM may not afford 256 MiB; for Tier W, C-07 runs Argon2id on the intake host per login, so 256 MiB × concurrent logins is an intake memory-exhaustion vector (THR-032). Suggest the ADR state a concurrency cap / admission queue for C-07 KDF operations or a lower server-side memory parameter compensated by the ≈129-bit passphrase entropy (F-020, F-025).
- **ADR-005 (per-deployment salt):** F-021 shows a shared salt enables all-verifier parallel guessing. With ≈129-bit generated passphrases this is not exploitable; the ADR should state this rationale explicitly so later changes to passphrase length do not silently weaken it.
- **ADR-001 (full vanguards via Python `vanguards` add-on):** R4 marks the add-on's 2026 maintenance status UNVERIFIED (F-059). ADR should name a fallback (vanguards-lite only, documented residual) if the add-on is unmaintained.
